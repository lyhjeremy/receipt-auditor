Receipt Auditor

Local LoRA fine-tune / benchmarked against Claude

A 1.5B model fine-tuned on a laptop sorts receipt line items into 12 categories at 90.5%

Receipt Auditor reads receipt photos, checks each total in code, strips personal data, and categorizes every line item with Qwen2.5-1.5B plus a LoRA adapter. Spending questions are answered by pandas; the language model only writes the sentence.

Top-1 accuracy, 12 categories

  • Base Qwen2.5-1.5B27.7%
  • Claude, zero-shot56.0%
  • Qwen2.5-1.5B + LoRA90.5%

220 held-out line items from 158 merchants not seen in training (Claude: 150 of them). Macro-F1 0.893 against 0.506. 4.5 s per item locally, 17.7 s for Claude. Synthetic data only, and the Claude figure understates it: the scorer took the longest category name in Claude's prose answers, and Claude could read the category list from the repo. The write-up goes through both.

How it works

  1. tesseract reads each photo; one claude -p call turns the text into a validated receipt or refuses a photo that isn't one.
  2. Line items plus tax are compared with the printed total, allowing the larger of 2 cents or 0.5%. A mismatch is flagged for review, never adjusted.
  3. The schema has no fields for card numbers, names or addresses; raw OCR text is discarded; a regex sweep redacts anything that slips through.
  4. Each line item goes to the LoRA classifier.
  5. A spending question is answered by a pandas query. The LLM phrases the result, and a guard checks every number survived, or falls back to a template sentence.
